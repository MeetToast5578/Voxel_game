# Spec 49 — Surface details and cell biomes

Complete T-E5, T-E6, T-E7, T-E8 and T-E10; finish procedural art T-E9.

- Boulders use seeded jittered anchors, rounded local rock and full-footprint support.
- Fallen logs use forest biomes, horizontal end-grain axes and flat dry support along their entire length.
- Lily pads are thin, noncolliding cutout plants above shallow still lake water; exclude rivers/oceans and freezing climates.
- Moss is a thin cutout layer on damp shaded rock and on damp boulders/fallen logs.
- Classify every surface cell using its actual elevation, climate and region moisture. Surface dithering follows nearby biome boundaries in world coordinates. Trees, ground cover and debug queries use the same cell biome.
- Keep old states stable; append states and procedural atlas tiles. Features evaluate whole footprints before clipping; no floating geometry, cave-mouth placement or terrain/tree replacement. Saved edits still apply after generation.

Validation: pure shape/ecology/support tests, horizontal/vertical/negative chunk clipping, stable state names and layer flags, real generated fixtures, cell climate classification, full check, strict worker generation/mesh budgets, GPU and gameplay smoke. Record limitations and results in log.txt.
