# Changelog

The newest version comes first. `npm run check` fails if the version at the top here is not the one in
`package.json`, so the two are changed together.

## 0.12.5 — 2026-11-11

Music for each place (roadmap R9.3).

- Each place has its own synthesised theme: bright by day, softer at night, low and slow in caves, dark and uneasy in
  the Ember Depths, lively in towns (yours or a village).
- A boss fight starts fast, low music at once, and it keeps coming back until the fight ends.
- Beating a boss plays a short rising fanfare.
- The music volume setting covers all of it.

## 0.12.4 — 2026-11-11

Gold gear (roadmap R6.1).

- **Gold** is a gear tier of its own, between iron and diamond: tools, sword, spear, hammer and armour from gold
  ingots. Gold tools dig fastest of all short of jade but wear out quickly (180 uses). A full gold set makes you 12%
  faster and gives 5% more critical hits.
- Diamond, jade and emberite need the same pickaxes as before.

## 0.12.3 — 2026-11-11

Challenge runs (roadmap R7.6).

- A new world can have a **goal**: beat a boss, reach the Ember Depths, light the beacon, or defeat the Deep Heart. A
  timer in the top right counts the time played (not paused time) and stops with a tick when the goal is met. The
  time is kept in the save, so a run can be shared by its seed and goal.

## 0.12.2 — 2026-11-11

The last two biome dungeons (roadmap R4.4).

- **Ice caves** in cold lands: a dome of ice half sunk in the snow, with a frozen chest. It always holds the
  **Glacier Charm** (keeps the cold off like warm clothes, 10% faster), with frost shards, diamonds and more.
- **Shipwrecks** on the sea floor, at least 6 cells under the surface: a planked hull with a broken mast and a chest in
  the hold. It always holds the **Captain's Compass** (breath lasts twice as long, 10% faster), with gold, coins and
  treasure maps.
- Treasure maps can show both.

## 0.12.1 — 2026-11-11

Two more townsfolk (roadmap R5.2).

- The **Tinkerer** moves into a free home once you have beaten a boss. They combine two accessories into one:
  - Cloud Charm + Featherfall Charm → **Storm Charm**;
  - Swiftstep Anklet + Dash Boots → **Sprint Boots** (dash, 25% faster);
  - Ironskin Band + Heart Locket → **Guardian Band** (stops 12% more harm, health returns twice as fast);
  - Ember Ring + Gill Pendant → **Elemental Amulet** (fireproof, four times the breath).

  They also sell Dash Boots and Grappling Hooks.
- The **Explorer** arrives once you have seen eight biomes. They sell **treasure maps**: reading one marks the nearest
  place not yet marked (a pyramid, temple, sky island, altar, shrine, tower or cabin) for the compass. They buy
  crystal shards, frost shards and dune fangs, and sell the Wisp in a Bottle.

## 0.12.0 — 2026-11-11

Towns under siege, and light in the dark (roadmap R3.5, R6.7, R2.4).

- **Town raids**: on half of all red nights, if you are near a town of at least two homes you built, eight monsters
  gather 30 m away and march on it, going for the townsfolk. Beat every raider for the townsfolk's gift: 30 coins and
  a Life Crystal. At dawn any raiders left slink away.
- **Wisp in a Bottle** (crystal shards, a glowing mushroom and a bottle): a wisp circles your head and lights the
  blocks around you.
- **Night Vision Potion** (a glowing mushroom and a crystal shard): see in the dark for two minutes.

## 0.11.0 — 2026-11-11

Caves with character (roadmap R4.1).

- Underground regions about 90 m across, from a little below the surface down to the Ember Depths, are cave biomes
  (two in five caves stay plain):
  - **Glowing mushroom caverns**: blue mushrooms light the floors. **Spore Slimes** (poisonous) live there.
  - **Crystal geodes**: violet crystals grow from floors and hang from ceilings, lighting the cave. Mining them gives
    crystal shards. Cave bats gather there.
  - **Spider nests**: cobwebs strung through the air hold you (a quarter of the speed, a slow fall); swords cut them
    for string. Cave crawlers live there.
  - **Flooded caves**: full of water from 15 m down; bring a Gill Pendant or a water breathing potion.
- Seed 1 has every kind within 300 m of spawn.

## 0.10.1 — 2026-11-11

The final boss (roadmap R4.2, R3.3).

- **The Deep Heart**: a floating heart of molten rock, 540 health, that sets you alight. Its named attacks: **Ember
  bolt** (dodge it, or wear something fireproof) and **Dive**. Fire Bats come to its aid.
- Summoned with the **Heart of Embers** (emberite ingots and ember crystals round a life crystal). It only stirs in
  the Ember Depths.
- Its treasure bag holds the **Deep Core** accessory: lava and fire do not burn you, health returns twice as fast,
  and it stops 9% more harm.

## 0.10.0 — 2026-11-11

The Ember Depths (roadmap R4.2, first part).

- Below about 220 m down the rock turns to **basalt**, seamed with glowing **ember stone**. The caverns open wide, with
  air above and **lakes of lava** below a lava sea at about 250 m down.
- **Emberite ore**, the new top ore, only in the Depths; only a jade pickaxe digs it. Smelt it into **emberite
  ingots**.
- **Emberite gear**, the sixth tier: tools, spear, hammer and armour, the strongest there are. A full emberite set
  makes you immune to lava and fire, and stops 6% more harm.
- **Fire Bats** (they set you alight) and Ember Slimes live in the Depths, instead of the usual cave monsters.

## 0.9.3 — 2026-11-11

A fifth boss (roadmap R3.3, third of three before the Depths).

- The **Sandmaw**: a great segmented worm, 380 health, whose bite makes you bleed. Its named attack, **Burrow**: it
  rears, sinks out of sight (nothing can hurt it underground), tunnels to beneath you and bursts up. Keep moving.
  Dune Stalkers come to its aid.
- Summoned with a **Sand Totem**: dune fangs and sand round a gold ingot. Dune Stalkers drop the fangs.
- Its treasure bag holds the **Dune Cloak** accessory: double-tap to dash, and 10% faster.

## 0.9.2 — 2026-11-11

A fourth boss (roadmap R3.3, second of three).

- The **Hive Mother**: a great hornet queen, 360 health, whose sting poisons. Named attacks: **Dive** and **Venom**
  (a spat stinger that poisons; dodge it). Jungle Hornets come to her aid.
- **Jungle Hornets** live in rainforests and swamps by day; their sting poisons and they drop Stingers.
- Summoned with **Royal Bait**: stingers and gel round a gold ingot.
- Her treasure bag holds the **Hive Charm** accessory: stings and venom never poison you, and you are 10% faster.
- The play-through's trade step holds the farmer in front of the player (it used to wander out of reach and fail).

## 0.9.1 — 2026-11-11

A third boss (roadmap R3.3, first of three).

- The **Frost Colossus**: a giant of ice, 420 health. Its bite slows you. Named attacks: **Stomp** (a shock round
  where it stands; step back) and **Boulder** (a thrown block of ice that hits hard and slows; dodge it). Its brood are
  Snow Prowlers.
- Summoned with a **Frost Sigil**: eight Frost Shards round a gold ingot. Frost brutes and Snow Prowlers drop the
  shards.
- Its treasure bag holds the **Frost Heart** accessory (stops 9% more harm, health returns 30% faster), diamonds, life
  crystals and more.
- Bosses are now described by data (their attacks, brood and summon item), so new ones need no special code.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-11-11

Fights you can read (roadmap R2.2).

- Every melee monster winds up before it bites: it crouches and flashes for a third of a second. Stepping back out
  of reach in that moment dodges the bite.
- Bosses have named attacks, announced on screen:
  - the Slime King's **Slam**: it crouches, leaps at you and hurts anyone on the ground near where it lands;
  - the Gloom Matriarch's **Dive**: it rises, then swoops at where you stood;
  - both keep calling their brood as they are struck.
- Below half health a boss is **enraged**: it turns redder and moves faster, its attacks come sooner, and it calls a
  brood of three at once.
- The shape gallery has a wind-up column.
- Development builds show uncaught errors on screen, with a Copy button for bug reports (R11.2).

## 0.8.5 — 2026-11-11

Easier to play, and something from the sky (roadmap R8.6, R8.7, R4.10).

- Accessibility settings: subtitles for sounds (with the direction of creature calls), reduce motion (no view kick and
  softer hurt flashes), and sprint and crouch that toggle on a press. Item rarity shows as a mark (• ◆ ★ ✦) as well as
  a colour.
- The first-steps hints go on further: torches, a furnace and iron, and building a first home.
- Meteors: once you have beaten a boss, a meteor sometimes falls at night 40–80 cells away. Its crater holds meteorite,
  hot to stand on, that only an iron pickaxe or better digs; the compass points to it. Meteor shards make the Star
  Staff (12 damage bolts) or sell to the merchant.

## 0.8.4 — 2026-11-11

Comfort and choices (roadmap R8.4, R6.3, R4.6, R6.5, R6.7).

- Chests: Take all, Deposit all (the whole backpack), Sort, and a name for each chest. Quick stack (in the inventory)
  puts backpack items into every chest within six cells that already holds that kind of item.
- Weapon modifiers: swords, axes, spears and hammers come out of crafting and chests with one — Keen (+10% crit),
  Heavy (more knockback), Swift (faster swings), Mighty (+15% damage) or, sometimes, Dull (−15%). The ember forge
  reforges a weapon for 5 coins. The modifier shows in the name and the tooltip.
- Waystones stand by cabins and towers. Touch one to wake it; at any woken waystone, travel to another for 2 coins.
- Markers: pin a spot by name from the pause menu (Journey); the compass points to the latest marker.
- A flower or a tuft of grass in front of a villager or an animal no longer stops trading with or feeding it.
- Glider Wings (found on sky islands): hold jump while falling to glide slowly and fast. Rocket Boots (leather boots,
  two ember crystals and gold): with your jumps spent, hold jump to climb for a moment. The Storm Charm combines the
  Cloud and Featherfall Charms.
- Pets: feed a wolf a bone and it follows you, fights monsters near you, catches up if left behind, and stays with
  you across saves. Your swings pass it by.

## 0.8.3 — 2026-11-11

Towns with something to say, and worlds that get harder (roadmap R5.3, R5.4, R5.5, R7.5, R7.6).

- Townsfolk talk: each says something fitting for their trade, the night, the rain or a red night, shown when you
  trade with them.
- Tasks: every townsperson has tasks, one after another: bring wheat, iron, gel, logs, gold, mushrooms or poppies;
  defeat zombies, skeletons, gloom moths, slimes or a mimic. Accept them in the trade screen, follow them in the
  journal, and hand them in for coins and goods (Dash Boots, a Featherfall Charm, a Life Crystal...).
- Furniture: chairs (facing you as placed), tables and candles (string over gel). A table counts for a home.
- Nights grow harder as a world ages: 2.5% more monsters per day, up to 60% more.
- Hardcore (one life): pick it for a new world; dying deletes the world. Its difficulty cannot be changed in the menu.

## 0.8.2 — 2026-11-11

A world that keeps going (roadmap R4.7, R4.8, R4.9; tasks 12.2, 12.3, 12.5–12.7).

- Fire: a flame block that spreads to wood, leaves, plants, wool and beds (never chests), licks through the air to
  the next thing that burns, creeps over grass leaving dirt, and burns out by itself. Rain from open sky and water
  beside it put it out. Lightning, moving lava and ember arrows start fires; standing in one sets you burning.
- Generated rivers flow: their water carries swimmers and boats downstream.
- Saplings grow into trees while their chunk is unloaded, as crops already did.
- The Ember Warden keeps its wounds: hurt it, leave, come back, and it is as hurt as before.
- Only chests that a structure placed hold loot; any other chest, also one placed in an old world, opens empty.

## 0.8.1 — 2026-11-11

A fuller combat kit (roadmap R2.5, R2.6, R2.8, R8.3).

- Spears and hammers in every tier (wood to jade). A spear reaches 60% further in a narrow thrust; a hammer hits
  hardest and knocks furthest but recovers slowly. Spear: a head on two sticks, diagonal; hammer: a head of three over
  two, on a stick.
- Throwing Knives (an iron ingot over a stick makes six): right click throws one. The Boomerang (three planks): right
  click throws it and it flies back to your hand.
- Arrows by kind: iron (+50%), ember (+30%, sets the target alight) and jade (double damage), each made from arrows and
  the material. The bow shoots the best arrows you carry, and the HUD counts them while it is held.
- Dash Boots (leather and feathers): double-tap a direction to dash, with a moment no bite can land.
- Dying no longer scatters everything: half the coins you carry drop where you fell, and you keep the rest. The death
  screen says how many.

## 0.8.0 — 2026-11-11

Places worth finding (roadmap R4.3, R4.4, R4.5, R3.1).

- Sky islands float 130 m up, about two per square kilometre: grassy discs on rocky undersides, each with a glass hut
  and a chest of sky treasure (always a Featherfall Charm; cloud charms, Life Crystals, gold). Reach them with a
  grappling hook, by building up, or with a double jump and a glide.
- Desert pyramids (in deserts and badlands) and overgrown temples (in rainforests, swamps and deep forests) each hold
  a chest with something found nowhere else: the Scarab Charm (10% faster, 6% more harm stopped) and the Jungle Idol
  (faster healing, longer breath).
- Mimics: some chests in vaults, pyramids and temples jump up as a monster when first opened. A mimic hits hard and
  drops gold and good accessories.
- Boss altars: round slate floors ringed by torch-lit pillars. A boss summoned on an altar leaves two treasure bags.
- Trees keep clear of altars, pyramids and temples. The creature gallery shows all 31 creatures.

## 0.7.6 — 2026-11-10

Events and boss rewards (roadmap R3.2, R3.4).

- Red Night: from the third day, about one night in eight brings a red moon. The sky and fog turn red, twice as many
  monsters come, and they often drop extra coins. It ends at dawn.
- Slime Rain: about one day in ten, slimes fall from the sky around you. Beat 25 and the Slime King comes for you.
- Bosses (the Slime King, the Gloom Matriarch, the Ember Warden) leave one treasure bag instead of scattered loot, so
  nothing is lost in lava or water. Right click opens it.
- Neither event happens on peaceful.
- Mouse look ignores the sudden jumps of half the window some browsers report under pointer lock, which could spin
  the view on a click.

## 0.7.5 — 2026-11-10

Towns the player builds (roadmap R5.1, R5.2).

- A bed you place makes a home once its room is one: closed all round (walls, floor and roof), at least 24 cells of
  space, with a door, a light, and a table or workbench. Placing the bed says what the room still needs. When it is a
  home, someone moves in and sleeps in that bed at night; they come back while the bed stands.
- Four new townsfolk with their own shops, each arriving when the time is right: the Guide (from the start: torches,
  seeds, a map and compass), the Merchant (once you carry 20 coins: arrows, a bucket, string, a Grappling Hook; buys
  gold and diamonds), the Nurse (after a Life Crystal: healing potions, antidotes, Life Crystals) and the Alchemist
  (once you have a bottle or potion: ingredients and potions). After them, farmers, smiths and herbalists move in.

## 0.7.4 — 2026-11-10

Getting around and dressing for it (roadmap R6.4, R6.2).

- Grappling Hook (three iron ingots and two string, at a workbench): right click throws it at a block up to 14 m away
  and pulls you there, then holds you against it. Jump to let go with a hop (air jumps come back); right click again
  takes it back. It lets go if the block breaks. A rope shows where it holds.
- Full armour sets add a bonus, shown in the tooltip and under Equipment: leather 10% faster on foot, iron 6% more
  harm stopped, diamond 10% more critical hits, jade hurts whatever bites you for 3.

## 0.7.3 — 2026-11-10

Reasons to keep going (roadmap R7.1–R7.3).

- Every journal milestone gives a reward, shown beside it in the journal: torches for the first wood and stone tools,

## 0.7.2 — 2026-11-10

Combat with more to it (roadmap R2.1, R2.3, R2.4).

- Skeletons are archers: they keep their distance, stop and raise their arms to draw, then shoot. Step aside during the
  draw and the arrow misses. Death messages name the shooter.
- Status effects, listed at the top left with the seconds left: poisoned, bleeding, slowed, burning, well fed, and the
  potion effects. Cave crawlers and Mire Hollows can poison, Frost brutes and Snow Prowlers slow, Dune Stalkers cause
  bleeding, Ember Slimes set you alight. A cooked meal leaves you well fed (health returns faster for 90 s).
- Potions, made at a new alchemy table (two glass bottles over three planks) from a glass bottle and two ingredients:
  Healing (red mushroom, gel), Regeneration (poppy, gel), Swiftness (dandelion, feather), Fire Resistance (ember
  crystal, coal), Water Breathing (raw fish, reed) and Antidote (brown mushroom, bone meal). Right click drinks one.
  A healing potion brings 30 s of potion sickness. Glass makes bottles; the herbalist sells bottles, healing potions and
  antidotes, and chests hold potions.
- The inventory's defense figure and armour tooltips use the new armour numbers.

## 0.7.1 — 2026-11-10

Checks that the game works end to end, and the fixes they found (roadmap R1).

- Any rock makes stone tools and a furnace: granite, basalt, slate and the other stones count as cobblestone. Worlds
  that start on granite could not make stone tools before.
- Sword hits pass through grass and flowers; only solid blocks shield a creature.
- Shift-click puts on armour (swapping what is worn) and accessories.
- Gear tiers are felt more: swords hit for 4, 5, 7, 10 and 13 from wood to jade (was 4 to 8). Armour stops 3% per
  point, up to 85% (was 4%, up to 80%), so jade armour now protects better than diamond.
- Monsters in a group never spawn inside a block, and dropped items rest at the edge of unloaded ground instead of
  falling through it.
- The boss health bar sits below the hint banner instead of over it. With an item held, the flat hand picture no longer
  flashes beside the 3D item.
- `npm run playthrough` plays a new survival world with real mouse and keys (trees, crafting, digging, fights, the
  Slime King, a trade, save and reload) and writes a screenshot report; CI runs it. `docs/balance.md` is a generated
  table of hits and bites per tier.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-09

The "3D Terraria" pass (Milestone 18).

- Crafting is a list, not a grid: everything you can make from what you carry, by category, with ingredients and
  have/need counts, Craft and Craft all. A workbench within four cells opens its recipes.
- New inventory: equipment and accessories, hotbar and backpack, and crafting side by side. Sort the backpack; a trash
  slot keeps the last item thrown away. Items have rarity colours.
- Five accessory slots and seven accessories: Swiftstep Anklet, Cloud Charm (double jump), Featherfall Charm, Ironskin
  Band, Gill Pendant, Ember Ring and Heart Locket. Life Crystals raise max health to 40.
- Creatures are rebuilt from smooth shapes on jointed skeletons with walk, run, graze, attack, flinch, hop and flap
  animation. Animals and the trader lie down to sleep; monsters never sleep.
- New monsters: green, cave and ember slimes that hop, cave bats that fly, and skeletons. Rare drops include
  accessories; monsters drop coins, and the trader sells accessories for them.
- Combat: swords and axes sweep an arc and hit everything in it, with crits, knockback and stagger, damage numbers,
  health bars and a slash trail. A brief moment of safety follows each bite. The held item is drawn in 3D and swings.
- A new top tier: Verdant Jade, deep below -200 m (diamond pickaxe needed). Jade tools mine fastest and hit hardest;
  jade armour is the strongest. Its art is recoloured from diamond's until authored art replaces it.
- Magic: the Spark Wand and Ember Staff cast bolts that fly straight, using mana (20, shown while a staff is held).
- Bosses: the Slime King (summoned with a Gel Crown) and the Gloom Matriarch (a giant moth, called at night with a Moth
  Lure); both call their brood when struck. Gloom Moths flutter over forests and plains at night.
- Villages: clusters of houses with pitched roofs in clearings. Each house has a farmer, smith or herbalist with their
  own shop, who wanders near home by day and sleeps in their bed at night. Defeated creatures fall over and fade.
- Automatic resolution keeps the frame rate smooth on slower machines (Settings, Resolution).
- Saves upgrade step by step, keeping a backup of the old record (save version 5).
